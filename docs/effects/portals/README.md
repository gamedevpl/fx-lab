# Evolution of Portals & Impossible Spaces (1994–2023)

Interactive WebGL2 analytical SDF ray-teleportation sandbox charting 30 years of non-Euclidean game geometry—from 1994 *Marathon* overlapping rooms to 2022 *Portal with RTX* light-through-portals and 2023 *Viewfinder* photo-to-3D reality.

Academic background: Seth Teller's 1991 portal visibility cells, *Portals and Mirrors* (1995), and Eric Lengyel's oblique near-plane clipping frustum (used in rasterized portal engines; here we teleport rays analytically through portal transforms in WebGL2 SDF raymarching so recursion, shadows, and reflections work cleanly in a single pass).

## 8 Eras & Verified Sources

1. **1994 — Overlapping Spaces (*Marathon*, 1994):** Two distinct rooms occupy the exact same 3D world coordinates behind a central column—crossing the seam transitions between a warm amber chamber and a cobalt sanctuary.
2. **1996 — Overlapping Sectors & Portal Mirrors (*Build / Duke Nukem 3D*, 1996 · *Descent* 1995):** Adds a classic Duke3D duplicated-room portal mirror plus a **Sector Glitch** toggle exposing Build's infamous overdraw artifact when two overlapping sectors are visible simultaneously.
3. **2006 — Fixed 3D Portals (*Prey*, 2006 · *Narbacular Drop*, 2005):** Arbitrary non-axis-aligned 3D portal windows letting you see and shoot through tilted alien corridors in real time.
4. **2007 — Recursive Player-Placed Portals (*Portal*, 2007):** Opposing Blue ↔ Orange portals produce an infinite curving corridor (toggle **Depth 1 vs 8**, or click anywhere to reposition the Orange portal!).
5. **2013 — Non-Euclidean Shifting Rooms (*Antichamber*, 2013):** Minimalist high-contrast architecture that silently rewrites itself behind your back—press **Look Away** to watch golden stairs vanish into floating crimson cubes.
6. **2018 — Seamless "Bigger on the Inside" Tunnel (*CodeParade*, 2018):** A tiny 1.8 m-deep exterior pavilion standing in a sunlit courtyard opens into a 22 m-long vaulted infinity hall. Toggle **Outside / Inside** or orbit around the box to verify its exterior depth!
7. **2022 — Ray-Traced Light & Shadows Through Portals (*Portal with RTX*, 2022):** Light beams, sharp companion-cube shadows, and glossy floor reflections pass straight through the Blue ↔ Orange portal aperture (implemented via WebGL2 SDF ray-teleportation).
8. **2023 — 2D Photo Becomes 3D Reality (*Viewfinder*, 2023):** Press **Stamp Photo** (or click) to project a floating 2D Polaroid directly into 3D space—slicing away the concrete room behind it and materializing real sunlit 3D sandstone columns and sky!
